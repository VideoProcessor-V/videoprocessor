1. Optical flow (docs/VP-nvof-ahead.md, "Built" and "Measured" sections)
   - The NVIDIA optical-flow engine is the established default motion source
     (optical_flow: on). Seed mode: the engine's block vectors replace the matcher's
     block search (6.3 ms of 11.8 ms GPU at 4K on the laptop); the matcher's
     refinement, half-pixel step and trust tests are unchanged. The engine alone is
     unsafe at cuts (corpus: the cut was warped) - not offered. Reach limit: engine
     vectors beyond the searches' reach are rejected.
   - Producer: its own D3D11 device and worker compute each consecutive pair up to 4
     frames ahead from the queued frames (half / quarter size luma decoded directly per
     format, threaded), into a VectorStore under the continuity generation. The
     de-judder takes its pair; miss -> engine at render time -> searches.
     Render-loop hand-off 0.010 ms; ~8 ms per pair on the worker (slow preset).
   - Queue: the Normal / Reliable default holds 4 ahead (target_frames 5); a time
     target holds at least 5 frames (low latency exempt); the queue wins conflicts.
   - Measured: synthetic corpus seed 99.8-100% of the matcher, cut held; 7 real clips
     seed (slow) 41.9% judder removed vs the matcher's 41.3%, damage 0.35 vs 0.40.
   - Fixed: a teardown fail-fast on NVIDIA (engine textures freed first); the old
     replace path ran the backward search over the engine's flow (precedence bug).

2. Source resolution
   - VBR detection: 16/32-px coding grids as signatures; starved frames are no
     evidence (detail above vs the stream's own lower-reading frames); axis agreement.
   - Real codecs (G18): 126 encodes, 0 native read lower, player-upscaled 1080 224/224;
     a 1080 master upscaled then coded at 4K is not detected (the encoder removes the
     signature; cliff and time-averaged coherence tried, neither separates) - a known
     limit (safe direction).
   - Look-ahead verification at cuts / black: wired (queued frames ahead).
   - Per-axis re-base: per-axis readings (single-axis coherence 0.5, rate <= 0.8),
     per-axis trackers (twice the shots and time), the re-base per axis (one axis: 1D
     Lanczos), WIDTHxHEIGHT manual class; aspect ratio held by the native output rectangle.
   - Kernel-matched re-base for 1080p (owner: the most common streaming case): the
     player's 2x kernel identified by reprojection residual (interpolating candidates
     only; strict floor/margin; votes over shots) and inverted on the GPU (luma, exact
     2:1). Real x265 decodes upscaled by ffmpeg: +8 to +15 dB closer to the decode than
     today's re-base; never worse in 8 real cases; rebase_kernel_match on|off.
   - Re-base cost at 4K on the laptop RTX 3050 Ti: 3.5 ms default (chroma separable,
     luma polar - a separable luma rang 2.2 codes below black into letterbox bars),
     2.1 ms with the kernel inverse; 3090 not yet measured.
   - Planner items: F12 rendition change (9.5 s, was 120 s), G1 periodic content
     native (three guards), G3 two-step lowest or native, G8 no thrash, G10 long dark
     film native, G11 freeze neither declares nor resets, G16 dithered effective bits,
     G17 clean low detail not "poor" (a real false alarm fixed: 10-bit band edges need a
     2-code step); G5 / G12 by design (docs/audit/detection-stress-tests-2026-10-16.md).

3. NEW since the first send - subtitles, layout, evidence time
   - Player-drawn subtitles (4K text over an upscaled 1080p picture) are real 4K
     detail. Without a guard: in a detection tile they made frames read native (63 % of
     subtitled frames), so a subtitled 1080p stream was never declared, and once
     declared a single such frame would switch the re-base off at once (bias to native).
     One frame with text in 4/8 tiles read 0.66 instead of 0.5 (never below the truth).
   - Fix, source_resolution_text_guard (Fixes under test, default on): a tile whose
     sharp steps (neighbours >= 1/3 of the range apart, >= 0.2 % of pixels) sit >= 80 %
     inside one band a quarter of the tile high is left out of that frame's reading.
     With it: every reading 0.5, the subtitled stream declared at 10 s, never thrown
     back. Real footage and the real-codec corpus: 0 tiles left out.
   - Subtitle sharpness through the re-base: both filters soften 4K text to about
     1080 and ring slightly (kernel inverse 99 codes RMS in the text, worst overshoot
     258; polar 113 / 175). Owner decision: legible is enough, no native-text overlay.
   - Layout at 4K with re-base on vs off: scope screen + touch inside, scope + NLS
     (stretch 1.32), letterboxed 2.39 + auto crop, all with edge blanking: picture
     bounds within 1 px, 0 px shift. NLS runs on the re-based grid: its stretched sides
     keep 91 % of the centre's relative sharpness (synthetic pattern).
   - Evidence time: owner set the standard to 3 shots and 10 s (was 20 s) at clarity
     0.8. Picture slider: Off (default) / Cautious 4 shots 20 s 0.9 / Standard 3 / 10 s
     / 0.8 / Fast 2 / 5 s / 0.7; per-axis needs twice the shots and time. All false-alarm
     tests (soft shots of a real film, VBR, alternating shots, real footage) pass at 10 s.
   - "Confidence" relabelled "upscale clarity" in Config (it is not a probability:
     evidence-complete x clarity, clarity = (mean min-axis mirror coherence - 0.4) / 0.3
     over the 120 s window; real 4K ~0, real upscales ~1), checked only after every
     frame in the window read lower and the shots/time agree.

4. Detail accumulation (docs/VP-detail-accumulation.md) - design, not built
   Measured on a real x265 stream with a streaming GOP, the I/B pulse is 3-4 points of
   fine detail / 1.5-2 dB; a CPU prototype of v1 (block motion, detail band, trust
   weight, clamp at any allowance) gains only +0.01 to +0.09 dB (gate +1 dB), and a
   strong weight makes I/P frames worse - the B-frames' coding loss is correlated with
   their references'. Proposed: do not build now; retest on real streaming captures
   (G19) and grainy sources.
