1. Optical flow (docs/VP-nvof-ahead.md, "Built" and "Measured" sections)
   - The NVIDIA optical-flow engine is the established default motion source
     (optical_flow: on). Seed mode: the engine's block vectors replace the matcher's
     block search (6.3 ms of 11.8 ms GPU at 4K on the laptop); the matcher's
     refinement, half-pixel step and trust tests are unchanged. The engine alone is
     unsafe at cuts (corpus: the cut was warped) - not offered. Reach limit: engine
     vectors beyond the searches' reach are rejected.
   - Producer: its own D3D11 device and worker compute each consecutive pair up to 4
     frames ahead from the queued frames (half / quarter size luma decoded directly per
     format, threaded), into a VectorStore under the continuity generation. The
     de-judder takes its pair; miss -> engine at render time -> searches.
     Render-loop hand-off 0.010 ms; ~8 ms per pair on the worker (slow preset).
   - Queue: the Normal / Reliable default holds 4 ahead (target_frames 5); a time
     target holds at least 5 frames (low latency exempt); the queue wins conflicts.
   - Measured: synthetic corpus seed 99.8-100% of the matcher, cut held; 7 real clips
     seed (slow) 41.9% judder removed vs the matcher's 41.3%, damage 0.35 vs 0.40.
   - Fixed: a teardown fail-fast on NVIDIA (engine textures freed first); the old
     replace path ran the backward search over the engine's flow (precedence bug).

2. Source resolution
   - VBR detection: 16/32-px coding grids as signatures; starved frames are no
     evidence (detail above vs the stream's own lower-reading frames); axis agreement.
   - Real codecs (G18): 126 encodes, 0 native read lower, player-upscaled 1080 224/224;
     a 1080 master upscaled then coded at 4K is not detected (the encoder removes the
     signature; cliff and time-averaged coherence tried, neither separates) - a known
     limit (safe direction).
   - Look-ahead verification at cuts / black: wired (queued frames ahead).
   - Per-axis re-base: per-axis readings (single-axis coherence 0.5, rate <= 0.8),
     per-axis trackers (6 shots, 40 s), the re-base per axis (one axis: 1D Lanczos),
     WIDTHxHEIGHT manual class; aspect ratio held by the native output rectangle.
   - Kernel-matched re-base for 1080p (owner: the most common streaming case): the
     player's 2x kernel identified by reprojection residual (interpolating candidates
     only; strict floor/margin; votes over shots) and inverted on the GPU (luma, exact
     2:1). Real x265 decodes upscaled by ffmpeg: +8 to +15 dB closer to the decode than
     today's re-base; never worse in 8 real cases; rebase_kernel_match on|off.
   - Planner items: F12 rendition change (9.5 s, was 120 s), G1 periodic content
     native (three guards), G3 two-step lowest or native, G8 no thrash; G5 / G12 by
     design (docs/audit/detection-stress-tests-2026-10-16.md).

3. Detail accumulation (docs/VP-detail-accumulation.md) - design, not built
   UPDATE (sections 11-12): measured on a real x265 stream with a streaming GOP, the
   I/B pulse is 3-4 points of fine detail / 1.5-2 dB; a CPU prototype of v1 (block
   motion, detail band, trust weight, clamp at any allowance) gains only +0.01 to
   +0.09 dB (gate +1 dB), and a strong weight makes I/P frames worse - the B-frames'
   coding loss is correlated with their references'. Proposed: do not build now; retest
   on real streaming captures (G19) and grainy sources.
   - Recurrent detail-band history warped by the de-judder's vectors; TAA-style clamp;
     v1 causal (refined field), v2 two-frame look-ahead with the engine's refined
     vectors; automatic disable without vectors / optical flow; detection reads the
     raw source first; tests T1-T7 incl. chroma formats and the capture-format caveat.
