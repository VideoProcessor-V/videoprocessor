# Scaling-chain estimator (`input.source_analysis (identity/chain/)`, SID-C1)

Pure units that find out from the picture lines alone whether, and from which size, the delivered picture was upscaled by the player, and with which kernel (doc 78 sections 2-4). Header-only C++14, deterministic. SID-C1 units return evidence and reason bits; the per-axis READING (`ChainReading.h`: confident vs UNSURE, no state machine) turns the evidence into what P14g publishes, with the P14g record and view (`../SourceGeometryProduct.h`, `../SourceGeometryView.h`) and the consensus voter (`../SourceIdVote.h`, `../SourceIdConsensus.h`); SID-C2b adds the worker that runs the units and publishes P14g (`ChainEstimator`), the SourceFrameTap sink, the CPU-share governor, the evidence log and the status texts (see "Estimator worker" below).

Unit: input/source_analysis (identity/chain)

## Products

P14g `source_geometry` (`../SourceGeometryProduct.h`: the skeleton's framework, product id 29, plus this build's record: held state, single writer, an 8 B header and a 371 B payload in the 512-byte bound, unit `SourceGeometry` v1; consumers read it only through `../SourceGeometryView.h` (U21): `Confident()` = the applied values of the axes that read CONFIDENT (not sticky: an UNSURE axis applies nothing), `Provisional()` = the measured, current best; absent / stale / other version / corrupt = the delivered geometry plus the engaged fallback `product.source_geometry`). `AxisReader` (`ChainReading.h`) produces the two geometries per axis; the states `Candidate` and `Unstable` of the unit are reserved numerics, never written (no unit bump). The estimator units return `VerifyResult`, `EvidenceUpdate` / `AxisSummary` and `ProbeResult` with reason bits (`kReason*` in `ChainCommon.h`).

## Status and fallbacks

Every abstention carries reason bits that C2 turns into the Ctrl+I line, the Config status and Ctrl+Alt+D: `LowDetail`, `Flat`, `Dark`, `Soft`, `NoPower`, `Mixed` (HOLD hint), `Masked`, `NoCandidates`, `ShortLines`, `NoReference`, `CoarseOnly`, `BadInput`. A native verdict is never produced without power, and a flat, dark or soft picture is UNSURE, never native.

SID-C2b turns them into the status line (`ChainStatusLine`, doc 78 section 7): "Source identity: 1080 -> 2160 lanczos-like + sharpening, confident", "native, confident", "H ... ; V ..." when the axes differ, "unsure (dark, flat)", "holding (overlay)", "acquiring, 7.2 of 15.0 nats" (UNSURE: the best hypothesis is below c1), "shadow" (suffix "[shadow: would-be, classical decides]"), "classical only (<reason>)" (estimator off, Original pathway, no frame tap, "frame tap lost" when the host removed the registration, an absent / stale record), "starting (no evaluation yet)", "stalled (no frames for N s)" when no evaluation finished for more than 2.5 x the planned interval + 1 s (a stopped worker, no frames, an unusable area: the reason is on the line, e.g. "active area too small for the lines"). No silent fallback: a dropped job is counted (newest wins), a frame without a source time or with no room for the lines is counted, a stretched cadence and an over-budget evaluation are named in Ctrl+Alt+D, a failing evidence disk is a notice.

## Reading (doc 78 section 5, owner decision 2026-11-07, `ChainReading.h`)

The chain is a DETECTOR, not a latch. One `AxisReader` per axis. `MakeFieldEvidence(summary, update, axisLen, settings, verify)` turns ChainEvidence's output into the reading's plain `FieldEvidence`; `Step(evidence, EvalContext{seq, timeUs, repeat, discontinuity, activeAreaChanged})` returns a `ReadingOutput` {state, `measured` and `applied` `Geometry::AxisGeom`, changed flags (state, measured, applied, kernel), `resetEvidence`, reason, reason bits}. The worker calls `ChainEvidence::ResetAxis` when `resetEvidence` is set.

- Per axis and evaluation: `n`, `N`; the reading is CONFIDENT when the best hypothesis is above c1 (`AxisSummary::aboveC1`; native: the native LLR above c1) on an informative evaluation, else UNSURE. UNSURE publishes NO applied value (nothing is sticky) and says why with a reason word: no evidence yet, below c1, axes disagree, mixed (HOLD), no power (dark, flat, soft, low detail), discontinuity. The kernel (index + confidence) is applied only when confident and the class is verified; otherwise "n known, kernel unsure". `measured` is the diagnostic best hypothesis whatever its strength; `applied` is the confident reading.
- States written to P14g: UNKNOWN, PROVISIONAL (a best hypothesis below c1), LATCHED (the reserved numeric of CONFIDENT; every text says "confident"), HOLD (> 20% of the informative lines contradict the majority: an overlay / PiP / menu), ABSTAIN (no power). `Candidate` and `Unstable` are reserved numerics, never written. c1 is a detection-confidence threshold from the null tables (`ComputeThresholds`); it adds no timing. Confidence of the applied axis = LLR / c1 capped at 1; since an axis is applied only at LLR >= c1 the confidence of an applied axis is exactly 1.0, so the consensus weight of a chain vote is exactly its trust (the conservative placeholder 0.5): a constant, not a graded weight, and calibration must not expect a gradient in it.
- PARKED (doc 78 section 5-parked, not built, borrow only through E5): the dwell, T_min, contrary-evidence timers, the regime switch toward native, the unstable state, c2. WHEN a re-base happens stays the original latch's business (engage moments, dwell, crossfade); WHAT it is told is the consensus's (chain + classical, `../SourceIdConsensus.h`).
- Recent window (N3, `ChainEvidence.h`): a hypothesis' closed shots expire after a window of SECONDS derived from the measured evaluation rate (`windowMargin` x c1 / `windowYieldNats` evaluation intervals, clamped to `windowMinSec`..`windowMaxSec`; the interval is a running mean of the source-time gaps), never a frame count, so an ABR rung change mid-play is not outvoted for ever by old evidence. The margin is 1.5 (revert window, owner-approved; it was 3.0): W = 1.5 x (c1 14.873 / yield 1.4) x the acquiring interval = 15.9 s at a 1 s interval, still derived in seconds from the measured rate. The accumulation of an axis is reset by a discontinuity (resync, format change, FULL era), an active-area change and a HOLD evaluation (an overlay / menu / PiP); see "Revert window" below for the other clears.
- Kernel equivalence class (F1): `DescaleVerifier` reports `CandidateResult::kernelMask` / `kernelT[]` (the T of every kernel it evaluated: the kernels of its best `exactFamilies` families). `MakeFieldEvidence` forms the class of kernels whose T is within `kernelClassMargin` of the best (at exactly 2x bilinear and bicubic are nearly the same operator) and picks the best class member the re-base can invert (it has a classical equivalent); a class without one, or a best kernel that does not dip at least `kernelDipMax`, is "kernel unsure". Without per-kernel T the family margin gives the confidence.
- A discontinuity or an active-area change = UNKNOWN (everything cleared, `resetEvidence`). A repeat changes nothing. Source time never steps backwards inside the reading.

## Revert window (owner-approved design; `ChainEvidence.h`, `ChainWindowPolicy.h`, `ChainEstimator.{h,cpp}`, `SourceResolutionMonitor.cpp`)

Detection-side only; WHEN (the original latch's engage moments, dwell, crossfade) is unchanged, the N2 hold of ONE native-looking frame is unchanged, `source_id_consensus` Off is the original byte for byte (the monitor feeds the events only with the consensus Shadow / On and the chain enabled). The aim: a source that genuinely becomes native at a moment where the old evidence must not outvote it is let go within a few seconds, not after the window.

The chain evidence window (both axes: `ChainEvidence::Reset()` plus the proposers, the path of a resync reset without the reader reset and without a reset record; the reading is a pure function of the evidence, so the evaluation after a clear reads UNSURE until new evidence builds) is CLEARED at:

| Trigger | Where it is detected | Status |
|---|---|---|
| resync / discontinuity / FULL era | `Monitor::Discontinuity`, the analysis gaps, `SetStreamKey` -> `ChainEstimator::NoteDiscontinuity` (full reset, reset record) | existing, unchanged |
| source format change | `Monitor::Frame` compares the format signature (size, representation, bits, transfer, primaries) and calls `Discontinuity("source format change")` | existing, unchanged (same path as a resync) |
| end of a HOLD / mixed (menu / overlay) episode | the monitor: the first analysed frame that is not mixed after one or more mixed frames (`FrameReading::mixed`) sends `EstimatorEvent::ClearWindow` (`WindowClearPolicy`); the chain's own HOLD evaluation (an overlay it reads on the lines) already forgets that axis's accumulation on every HOLD evaluation (`ChainEvidence`, N3) | new (monitor event) + existing (the estimator's own HOLD) |
| the first cut after a HOLD / mixed episode | the monitor: a cut on a frame that is not mixed once an episode happened (`WindowClearPolicy`, same `ClearWindow` event); the estimator: the first consumed cut after one of its own HOLD evaluations (`ChainEvidence::NoteCut`, both axes) | new |
| frame-rate change | NOT wired: see below | open |
| cadence change | NOT wired: see below | open |

Black / dark frames (the Blank event, a flat or dark evaluation that abstains) and ordinary cuts do NOT clear (tests: `ChainEvidenceTests`, `ChainWindowPolicyTests`, `ChainRevertBoundTests`).

Frame-rate and cadence changes are not observable in the monitor: it gets only the source seconds per frame (the renderer passes the steady clock of the render call: `sourceMonitor.Frame(..., now)`), and measures `secondsPerFrame` (an EMA) in `AutoFactor` as the pan speed's time base; display pacing (24p on a 60 Hz output) makes that interval alternate, and the monitor never sees repeats (`NoteRepeat` has no caller). A detector on that interval would clear the window (and so end the chain's N2 protection of the lock) on pacing noise. A real frame-rate change that arrives with an HDMI mode change (Apple TV match frame rate) is a resync and clears through the discontinuity path above. What is wired for the rest: `EstimatorEvent::ClearWindow` is the entry; a call site that knows the source rate or the pulldown cadence changed (the renderer's cadence detector) only has to call `NoteEvent(ClearWindow, seq)`.

Measured end to end by `ChainRevertBoundTests` (the real monitor with the consensus On and the real estimator on a confident 540 -> 1080 upscale that becomes genuinely native at the trigger; every test prints the measured times): after a menu end, after the first cut following a menu episode, and at a format change the consensus lets go within 2 x acquiring interval + 2 x frame interval = 3.0 s (the trigger is seen on its frame, the estimator consumes the event at its next evaluation, the reading is re-made from fresh evidence, the monitor reads it on its next frame). A black frame does not clear: the window survives (the first evaluation after it reads the upscale confidently again at once), and a switch to native after it takes the window case, W + 2 x acquiring interval + 0.5 s = 18.4 s (the former bound with W = 31.9 s was 34.4 s). An ABR rung change with no event (`ChainEvidenceTests`, evidence level, no contrary evidence): the old rung stops reading confidently and the new one is followed within W + 2 intervals (the test prints both times). A single native-looking frame mid-scene never clears the lock (N2): `SourceResolutionChainClearTests` (monitor level) and `NativeLookingFramesMidSceneNeverLetGoOfAConfidentUpscaleWithoutATrigger` (end to end).

### Long takes: what the shorter window costs (plain)

c1 is 14.873 and a decisive but hard 8-bit grainy picture gains about 1.4 nats per evaluation (F0), so c1 needs about 10.6 evaluations. The window used to hold 31.9 evaluations at a 1 s interval (margin 3.0); it now holds 15.9 (margin 1.5): 1.5 times what c1 needs, 10.6 needed against 15.9 in the window. Because one shot is capped at c1/2 (7.44), c1 can only be reached with TWO shots inside the window. Consequence for a long take (no cut): the shot gathered before the take falls outside the window W after its last evaluation, and the take's own open shot can never exceed c1/2 alone, so after about 16 s without a cut the reading falls from confident to UNSURE (the evidence is not lost for good: the next cut, which starts a new shot, brings it back within the evaluations that fill two shots). While the reading is UNSURE the chain does not hold native-looking frames (N2) and does not vote in the consensus: the classical detector alone decides, as it did before the chain existed. For a 60 s take on a 540 -> 1080 upscale the chain protects the lock for roughly the first 16 s after the last cut and not after; with the old window it was roughly 32 s. If long takes must be protected for longer, the margin (hidden tuning `windowMargin`) is the knob, at the price of a slower let-go for an ABR change without an event (W) and a longer W case after a black frame.

## Estimator worker (SID-C2b, `ChainEstimator.{h,cpp}`)

```
SourceFrameTap sink "chain" (frame-analysis worker)  -> copies only the LineSampler lines (integer codes, ~0.4 MB at 4K), releases the plane
   -> bounded queue (1-2 jobs, newest wins, counted) -> worker thread: proposer -> verifier -> power probe -> ChainEvidence -> AxisReader x2
   -> Geometry::HeldState::Publish (P14g, single writer) -> evidence log (worker-side file I/O only)
```

- Due predicate: the sink registers with `SourceFrameTap::Register(owner, "chain", sink, wants)`; `wants` is true only when `now >= nextDue` (the governor's interval), so the Host decodes a plane for it only when an evaluation is due. The old single-sink API is the name "", unchanged (the skeleton's analysis lane): `Wants(owner)` is true for any sink of the owner that wants a frame, `Deliver` hands the frame to every sink in name order, `Unregister(owner)` removes only the name "" (a module's own teardown), `UnregisterAll(owner)` is the Host's. The skeleton may use `Registered(owner, "")` to learn that ITS sink was lost (`Wants` is also true while the chain sink is due).
- Source time and active area come from `NoteFrame(seq, seconds, x, y, w, h)` (the monitor's per-frame call on the render thread: a short leaf lock, nothing else); a frame beyond the newest entry is extrapolated from the newest two, else skipped (`noClock`). Events: `NoteEvent(Cut | Blank | Fade | ClearWindow, seq)` are consumed by the first evaluation of a frame with seq >= the event's (each cut is a shot for ChainEvidence's per-shot cap, the flags go to the log; ClearWindow clears the evidence window before that evaluation and is counted in `windowClears`, see "Revert window"); `NoteRepeat(seq)` skips a cadence repeat without using the due slot; `NoteDiscontinuity` / a stream-key change / a format change clear everything and publish a reset record (`discontinuities` bumps); an active-area change restarts the reading at the first frame of the new area.
- Deterministic: the record bytes are a pure function of the job sequence (lines, source time, consumed flags); wall-clock reads only decide WHEN an evaluation runs and the reported cost. A dumped ring replays exactly (`SubmitLines`, tested).
- Modes: Off (no work, sink unregistered, held record reset), Shadow (published with `kFlagShadow`, logged; the classical decision stands), On. The Original pathway forces Off. One persistent worker thread, created the first time it runs, joined by `Stop()` / the destructor; start / stop / reset never wait for an evaluation.
- Governor (`ChainGovernor.h`): interval = max(nominal, costMs / share); nominal 1 s while acquiring and 5 s while both axes read confident; share 15% acquiring, 2% confident. `costMs` is the wall time of the evaluation (cold evaluations do not stretch the cadence once a warm one was seen); an evaluation above 250 ms is flagged and the interval is stretched, visibly in Ctrl+Alt+D. Confident: 16 + 16 lines and the exact stage on 16 lines (a confident n always gets it). N2 bound (round 1): the confident regime (5 s, 2 %) ends while the monitor keeps leaving native-looking informative frames out on the strength of the estimator's own record: `NoteNativeHeld(axisMask)` clears the regime, an evaluation already due at the long interval is pulled forward to one ACQUIRING interval after the last start (`acquiringIntervalMs`, the governor's cost-aware plan), and every evaluation that ends with a note since the previous one uses the acquiring plan; with no note for one interval the confident cadence returns. No new fixed number. The evidence window (N3) follows the measured, shorter interval.
- Output step: 4 when every sampled code is a multiple of 4 (an 8-bit picture carried in 10-bit codes), else 1 (`outputStepOverride` forces it); the floor handed to the evidence stage is 1.155 / 0.289.
- Monitor wiring (`SourceResolutionMonitor.h`): default OFF, byte-identical to before until `SetChainEnabled(true)`; then the monitor drives the mode from `SetSourceIdMode`, feeds `NoteFrame` (Frame), cuts / blanks / the classical decision (Analyse), discontinuities (`Discontinuity` and the analysis gaps) and the stream key, wires its geometry source to the estimator's holder, and exposes `GeometryView()` (confident + provisional on the frame clock), `ChainStatusLine()`, `ChainDetailText()`, `TakeChainNotice()`. The status needs no new display call (it rides on the existing `Describe()` line, see the Environment table). No existing call hands the monitor its renderer identity, so while `SetChainTap` was not called, `Frame()` finds the owner itself (once a second): the only renderer with a registered store (`SourceFrameTap::StoreOwners()`, filled by the existing `frameAnalysis.Owner(this)`); with none or several, nothing is attached and the status says "no frame tap". Call sites still needed in Bill's renderer (M2 P11): the `source_id_consensus` / `source_id_evidence` key plumbing (`SetSourceIdMode` + `SetChainEnabled`, `SetChainEvidence`; until then the two VP_SOURCE_ID_* switches), the report-a-problem hotkey (`Monitor::SetOriginalPathway` already forces the mode Off and the chain follows), optionally `SetChainTap(this)` (exact owner instead of discovery) and the Ctrl+Alt+D detail text.

### Evidence log (`ChainEvidenceLog.{h,cpp}`, doc 78 section 9b)

Worker-side file I/O only; off in the Original pathway, when the estimator is off or the level is `off`. Rows (JSON lines): one per SEGMENT (the span between discontinuities and changes of the confident size: wall and source times, input format, active area, time in each state per axis, final measured / applied values, LLR, top-3 candidates, power-probe seen share, line-gate share, spatial masked share, classical ratio vs the chain's would-be ratio with a disagreement flag, cost mean / p95; tier, ABR events, would-be notices and the quality-pattern summary are `null` until SID-E), one per state change (full detail), one aggregate row per 10 s of source time, one per trigger. RAM ring of the last 30 evaluations' lines, dumped (uncompressed binary + JSON header, `SerializeDump` / `ParseDump`) on a trigger at level log+lines and above; at most 4 evaluation frames beside it at level log+lines+frames. Triggers: a confident reading that disagrees with the classical decision, HOLD for more than 60 s, power unseen in more than 80% of the probes over 5 minutes, a coverage bucket (ratio class x kernel family x bit depth x content class x sharpening; the content class and the sharpening are placeholders until the harness / SID-C3) below its target count (`coverage.txt` keeps the counts), the hotkey. Rate limits: <= 1 dump per 5 minutes and <= 20 per 24 hours; the hotkey is exempt from both (5 s debounce). A press that cannot dump is never silent and never kept for later: while the estimator or the log is off, at level `log` (no lines are kept) or inside the debounce, a one-time notice says why ("evidence dump ignored (...)"). Cap: 2 GB in `sid-evidence`, oldest file first (FIFO), the open log file is never evicted; the cap is enforced AFTER each write, so the folder can overshoot it by one file (at most about 70 MB at 4K with frames). The day budget (20 dumps) is per process: a restart resets it, the 2 GB cap still bounds the disk.

### SID-C2b against the C2a framework (b09e6f43)

The estimator publishes a record after EVERY evaluation (abstain, hold and repeat included: a published record is the proof it is alive), `maxAgeUs = SourceGeometry::MaxAgeUs(the PLANNED interval)` (the governor's stretched interval) and each consumed cut event is a shot (`shotId` advances by it). A discontinuity publishes the reset record immediately with `evalSeq` = ONE PAST the newest frame the monitor had announced or evaluated (the era's first sequence: never below the monitor's floor, which is the row sequence after its last analysed frame; a second call right behind the first keeps the first call's clock) and `discontinuities` bumped; every record is stamped with the stream key of the snapshot it evaluated. `MakeFieldEvidence` gets the verifier result (kernel class). The Original pathway reaches the estimator through `Monitor::SetOriginalPathway` (mode Off). `Monitor::GeometryView()` reads through `ReadGeometry` and the trailing (Consumer, frameSeq, readCause) parameters.

### SID-C2b round-2 audit fixes, timing limits and open items

- Events and the newest-wins queue: a job replaced by a newer one hands the events it consumed to its replacement (flags, cuts, event time), so the shot count and the flags in the log see them. While BOTH axes read confident (the 5 s cadence) a cut / blank / fade pulls the next evaluation forward to the next frame (at most once per second), so a boundary is evaluated at the cut, not seconds later.
- EVENT TIMING LIMIT: the monitor reports a cut or blank from its tile-cadence analysis worker, after the sink may already have evaluated a frame at or beyond the event's sequence. That evaluation carries no flag; the NEXT evaluation does (acquiring: within about 1 s; confident: pulled forward as above). The readings are not timed by events any more (`LineSnapshot::eventTimeUs` stays in the dump format, reserved, written -1), so the limit only shifts a shot boundary by one evaluation (test `ALateEventIsCarriedByTheNextEvaluation`). A bounded "analysed through" wait was not added: it would hold the render-side delivery on the analysis worker.
- Blank is an EDGE (the monitor feeds it on the transition to a non-informative frame, not on every dark frame: a level-triggered flag restarted the contrary runs and restarted the shot accounting). FADE IS NOT FED: the monitor has no fade source today (`fade` column 0 in the history CSV).
- Gate clock: the chain records are stamped in the frame's RAW source seconds (as the estimator and P14 do); `AutoFactors` hands the chain gate those raw seconds, not the never-backwards decision clock, so a backwards seek no longer makes every record read stale for the session.
- Open items (not fixed in this package): (1) the evidence ring crosses discontinuities: `OnDiscontinuity` keeps the ring and `kEvDiscontinuity` is never written into a snapshot, so a dumped ring reproduces the live record bytes only when it begins at a reset or a clean start (a ring of 30 evaluations that starts mid-era cannot rebuild the evidence accumulation); (2) `SkeletonModule` uses `Wants(host)` as "my sink is registered" (true while only the chain sink wants) and `VpFrameAnalysisHost::AttachIfEnabled` attaches a plane to every prepared source whenever any sink exists, even when the chain is not due; (3) a frame dump (`SerializeFrame`, 4 x 16 MB at 4K) blocks the worker for about 1 s inside its processing lock: in the acquiring mode (maxAge 2 s) the record can read stale for that second (visible, not silent); (4) some tests assume a pristine process-wide tap registry (`Tap::AnyWants()`, `StoreOwners().size()`) and an `Assert` on the reader thread of the threading test ends the process instead of failing.

## Method (analysis by synthesis)

For a candidate original size n of an N-pixel axis and a kernel k, every sampled line is shrunk back to n samples by least squares ("descale") and blown up again with the same kernel. If the picture really was upscaled from n by k, almost nothing is lost (rounding noise only); a native picture always loses its finest detail. Per line the statistic is `v = ln(s(n,k) / baseline)`, `s = sqrt((ms + eps^2) / dof)` with `ms` the mean square residual over the inner rows and `dof = 1 - nodes/rows` (the unknowns of the fit; natives then sit at 0), the baseline being the median of `s` at the reference sizes n(1 +- 4%), n(1 +- 8%) (smallest over the four representative kernels). The candidate statistic `T` is the 20%-trimmed mean of `v` over lines (negative = a dip at n). Mapping, centre aligned: `p = (i + 0.5) n / N - 0.5 + phase`.

### Proposer: polyphase predictor gain (deviation from doc 78 section 3, accepted by the lead)

Doc 78 asked for the periodogram of the squared residual of ONE pooled predictor. On synthetic chains with the final rounding that statistic did not work: for the commonest case, a centre-aligned 2x upscale, the two phases are mirror images and a pooled (symmetric) predictor has the SAME residual variance at both, so e^2 shows no periodicity at all (it also vanished for 3x, where the residual is pure rounding noise); a causal 5-tap predictor saw 1/2 and 2/3 but only weakly 1/3 and 5/12. The proposer therefore fits the 5-tap predictor PER PHASE CLASS (period q = 2..16, phase = pixel index mod q, neighbours -3..+2, line mean removed) and compares the summed residual with the pooled one: `gain = N ln(RSS_pooled/RSS_q)`, `ratio = gain / (5(q-1))`, `dBIC = gain - 5(q-1) ln N`. On the synthetic prototype natives stayed below ratio 3.6 and chains reached 15-2700; `ratioMin` is 5 and the test logs the largest native ratio and asserts it stays below 4.5. A period q says the ratio is m/q with m coprime to q; every such n = round(N m / q) is a candidate (nearest 1/2 first inside a period). The slots are filled by strength: periods in dBIC order, one size per period per round, after the caller's sizes. `lowVarPhase` reports the phase class with the smallest residual variance. Only sufficient statistics are kept, so evaluations accumulate at no extra cost (the phase class of a sample is a rolling counter, no division per sample). Limits: a ratio needing a period above `qMax`, or an N m / q that is not an integer (the phase then drifts along the line), is only found through the caller candidates (previous latched n, rationals of the active size).

### Verifier (deviations from doc 78 section 3, measured; accepted by the lead)

- The coarse scan is NOT step 4: on a window of N/8 rows at the START or END of the lines the notch is alias free and about +-1.5 integers wide (a centred window shows a comb of aliases at every even offset), so it scans every integer within +-4 (step 1) on both windows. It refines the candidate and ranks candidates.
- The exact stage uses the full lines (or `VerifyOptions::exactLines`, 16 when latched): integers n^ +-1 with the representative kernels, then at the best integer every kernel of the best two families (optionally at several sub-sample phases). Only the best `maxExactCandidates` (2) candidates AND the caller's latched size (`forceExactN`) get it; the rest are reported coarse-only. A kernel is judged by its T against the SAME baseline (the smallest residual wins). At exactly 2x bilinear and bicubic are nearly indistinguishable (F0 saw the same); tests accept either there.
- All kernels use the same inner rows (margin 1.5 supports of a radius-4 kernel at each window end) so they compare like for like. Phases are quantised to 1/1024 before an operator is built, tap weights to 2^-30, so the cache never changes a result.
- `CandidateResult::floorCodes` is the RAW rms residual at the best (n, kernel); `AxisObservation::floorCodes` (the gate input) is the dof-normalised rounding/dither sigma, 1.155 for an 8-bit output and 0.289 for 10-bit. They are different quantities.

### Evidence

Per candidate: the line gate (baseline >= 1.5 x the rounding/dither floor on at least 8 of 32 lines, else `kReasonLowDetail`; the gate is a cliff because baselines of one picture cluster within about 8%, and 3 x demanded more detail than any 8-bit output of this synthetic content has), spatial agreement (lines at a robust distance from the median in the opposite direction are masked below 20%, above it the candidate is mixed; the per-256-px segment map is judged the same way along the line, informative segments only). Only the TOP hypothesis (the informative candidate with the lowest median line value) can hold the evaluation: if it is mixed the evaluation is a HOLD hint `kReasonMixed` and nothing accumulates; a wrong candidate that is mixed is just dropped. Then the LLR increment of the masked T from the `NullTables` class of n, clamped to [-3, +5]. Exact sizes within +-1 of each other are one hypothesis (the best per evaluation counts; the hypothesis value follows the latest best exact size), so refinement jitter cannot split the evidence. The native hypothesis only earns evidence when the power probe SAW its injection (otherwise `kReasonNoPower` and 0). Per shot (id from the caller, bumped at every cut) the summed increments are capped at c1/2, so c1 needs at least two saturated shots (two exactly reach it). Two axes: when both best hypotheses are positive, agreeing ratios add +2 nats to each and disagreeing ones subtract 2; a silent axis changes nothing. `c1 = -ln(alpha) + ln(candidates x kernels)`, `alpha = rate/hour x 10 s / 3600` (about 14.9 for 1 per 100 h) (there is no c2: nothing is sticky).

NullTables are PLACEHOLDERS (conservative: wide and deep nulls where natives are known to dip, a wide shallow alternative, clamped increments). They can only make the estimator abstain more than calibrated tables would; the calibration run writes `NullTables.generated.h` (same struct). With them a decisive chain reaches c1 within a few evaluations over two to three shots, a weak grainy dip never does.

### Power probe

The sampled lines are upscaled at ratio 0.61 (bicubic, rounded to the delivered output step) and the verifier is run on that with the known latent size N. Seen (T <= -0.15 on the informative lines) = a "native" verdict of the real candidates is positive evidence. Unseen = abstain with the reason: dark (mean level), flat (almost no line structure), soft (structure but no fine detail above the floor). A really upscaled picture is usually unseen by the probe (its detail stops at the latent Nyquist): that is fine, its dip evidence needs no probe.

### Cost (estimate, NOT measured; one thread, 3840x2160, +-2x; the timing test logs the real numbers)

One full line solve is about 40 us at 3744 samples. Per evaluation (both axes), coarse stage on 8 lines and probe on 16 lines: latched, one candidate, exact stage only on 16 lines: about 63 ms cold and 40 ms warm (operator cache) including the probe; acquiring, 3 candidates, exact stage on 32 lines: about 270 ms cold and 190 ms warm (proposer 16, coarse 3 x 9 x 2, exact 2 x (60 + 33), probe 16). The result reports its own cost: `VerifyResult::costMs`, `cold`, `operatorBuilds`, `solves` (and `ProbeResult::costMs`, `cold`), with the wall time read through the optional `VerifierSettings::nowMs` hook (the units never read a clock; C2 passes steady_clock). A CPU-share governor in C2 sets the interval to max(nominal, cost / share budget): at 15% while acquiring that is about 1.3 to 1.8 s, at 2% latched about 2 to 3 s, shown in Ctrl+Alt+D. Cache memory is about 70 MB at 3 candidates per axis (`cacheLimit` 512).
