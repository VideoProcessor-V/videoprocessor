# CutFadeJudge (shared unit U7, `VpUnit::CutFadeJudge`)

The one cut / fade / flash decision (doc 62, section 1.2 U7): `CutFadeJudge.h`, `kUnitVersion = 3` (2: the U7-ACCURACY sequence cut law; 3: the multi-frame fade law and the partial reset on fades). Bill's SceneDetector stays the Original pathway and is untouched.

- `ClassifyEvents` (moved unchanged from the lane): two P4 records -> cut / "cut possible" / fade / flash; `StrengthOf` maps it to the Indicator strengths (FULL = cut, MINOR = cut possible).
- `ScorePair` / `CutTracker` (unit version 2, U7-ACCURACY, the T2 sequence cut law): the cut over the P4 32 x 18 cell grids of frames k-4 .. k+1 (a local-motion-tolerant residual spike against its temporal context, structure kept = a lighting change, a return at k+1 = a flash); one frame late, provisional without k+1; no grid: the pair law (counted). Its thresholds and the `U7CutTruthGateTests` gate (recall and precision >= 99.5 %, zero cuts on pan / flash / fade) are unchanged by version 3.
- `FadeRun` + `FoldFadeRun` (unit version 3, the multi-frame fade law): the pair law needs a mean step of spread / 64 per pair, so a slow fade (a 2 s fade at 24p moves about 1/100 of the spread per pair) was found on only its last frames. The caller keeps one `FadeRun` per stream and folds every pair in order; the frame's fade = the pair law's fade OR the run's. A run = consecutive pairs whose p99 moves one way (p99 holding stays in the run; a cut, a flash, a reversal or a mean jump >= spread / 4 ends it; a repeated record leaves it as it is). A pair in a run of >= 2 pairs (`kFadeRunMinPairs`) is a fade when its mean and p99 moved the run's way and the run from its first record to this one is a gain about a plausible black: the black implied by mean and p99 lies in FadeFit's window (`kFadeMinBlack` .. `kFadeMaxBlack`, 8-bit-like codes, x `kP4CodesPerEightBit`) and |1 - gain| > `kFadeRunMinGain` = 0.02. A run continues only from the record it ends at (`FadeRunEndsAt`), so a skipped frame or a restarted numbering starts a new run. Mean and p99 (not p01): p01 jitters by several bins on dark pictures (the sub-black tail). Cut, "cut possible" and flash are unchanged. Producer: the skeleton's scene product (`SkeletonModule::SceneFactsOf`, a ring of the run per frame, rebuilt from the lane's older P4 records when an entry is missing; a rebuild with reduced evidence is counted and shown).
- Partial reset on fades (unit version 3, owner 2026-11-15 09:20, refs\102b U7-FADE-NO-HARDRESTART): a fade is NOT a restart. `FadeRun::keep` = min(gain, 1 / gain) of the run about its fitted black, never rising inside a run; `FoldFadeRun(..., &frameKeep)` gives the frame's step (keep after / keep before). `PairFadeKeep` = the pair law's spread ratio (min / max of p99 - p01). `FrameFadeKeep` = the smaller factor of the laws that called the frame a fade, 1 on a frame that is no fade. The product over a fade is the contrast the pre-fade picture still explains (a fade to black: 0), so consumers that multiply their history by it each frame shrink it monotonically and carry no pre-fade detail into the new scene. Amplitude domain; energy holders apply its square. No frame count, rate or window: the measured contrast change of the frame. Published as `FrameAnalysis::Scene::fadeKeep` (< 0 = not given: a consumer then falls back to the full restart, visibly). A full restart stays for the delivered cut only.
- Soft reset rule (C5 batch B, `cut_soft_reset`; additive, no output of the unit changes, so `kUnitVersion` stays 3): `SoftResetStrength(confirmed, candidate, cut, flash, fade, strength)` = the strength when the CONFIRMED verdict is a candidate that is neither cut nor flash, the frame is no fade frame, and `strength >= kSoftResetBar`; else 0 (below the bar: no reset). `SoftResetKeep(s)` = 1 - `kSoftResetDepth` x s, the history keep a consumer multiplies by (amplitude). `kSoftResetBar` = 1.0 and `kSoftResetDepth` = 0.5 are PROVISIONAL pending the D8 truth run. With the T2 strength min(1, L / bar_T2), every spike candidate has strength 1, and the only candidates that are neither cut nor flash are those vetoed by "no cut right after a flash"; so at bar 1.0 only such a veto whose residual fully passed the spike bar fires. Consumer: `SceneRestart.h` (detail pulse, texture restore).
- `GridHistCut` + `MeanAbsDifference`: the HDR veto detector's rule; its thresholds (`kGridDifference`, `kHistogramDistance`, `kHistogramDistanceAlone`) are the single source and `HdrVetoCutDetector` aliases them.
- `FadeFit`: CPU twin of the matcher's M4 fade law with its switch in `FadeParams::enabled`. The law's numbers are named constants (`kFadeMinStepCodes`, `kFadeMaxStepCodes`, `kFadeMinBlack`, `kFadeMaxBlack`, `kFadeScaleEps`, `kFadeGain*`, ...); the matcher takes its per-frame step window from `FadeParams` (scaled to the measured source rate, never a literal rate).
- GLSL (moved byte-for-byte from the consumers; the consumers splice or dispatch the unit's text): `FadeKernelHeader` / `FadeKernelBody` (the matcher's M4 fade test, `DejudderWarp.cpp`), `TrustCutRegionalLine` / `TrustCutPlainLine` (the matcher's frame-trust cut; the histogram sweep it reads is `SourceStats::CoarseHistBody`, U6), `MeanStepCutLine` (formerly AdaptiveDownscale's scalar-pass cut; NO consumer since FIX-X5-CUT-P4; kept while `SharedUnitsW1Tests` and the shared-units baseline still reference it, retirement to be recorded as a 102b row). CPU twins: `FadeFit`, `TrustCut`, `MeanStepCut` (likewise unconsumed).
- Law of the two added cuts: matcher trust cut = share of trusted blocks below the minimum share AND (coarse histogram distance above the cut threshold OR share below `kTrustCutShareAlone` = 0.05); without the regional hold the share alone. Mean-step cut (no live consumer, see above) = no state yet, or the frame's mean luma (8-bit codes) moved more than `kMeanStepCutCodes` = 20. AdaptiveDownscale's grain-term cut reads the skeleton scene product's delivered cut (`FrameAnalysis::Scene::cut`; FIX-X5-CUT-P4, moved in C5); it calls no `ClassifyEvents` and never snaps on a fade.
- Identity (unit version 1, W1-SU): no unit version bump then (additive, byte-identical). The assembled matcher and AdaptiveDownscale kernels were proven textually identical to the base kernels; the fragment hashes are pinned in `SharedUnitsW1Tests` (`ResidualKernelTextIsTheTextTheConsumersCarried`) and the existing GPU tests (`DejudderHookTests`, `EstimatorHookTests`, the AdaptiveDownscale GPU tests) are the behavioural identity gate.
- Not migrated: SRM `FrameCutJudge`, LED look-ahead and the SA3 reader switch come later. The matcher's fade APPLICATION (B x gain + offset in its half-luma pass, reading `fade_state`) is a consumer of the fade state this unit writes, not a second judgement.

## Products
P4 event (cut / fade / flash are derived from the P4 records; the skeleton's scene product stores the cut / fade verdict once per frame in the frame-analysis store). Unit version 3; consumers: lane classifier, HDR veto detector, the skeleton scene product (read by detail_pulse_stabilise and texture_restore through `SceneRestart.h` (cut = restart, fade = partial reset by `fadeKeep`), the content-intent observers and the camera extension through the store, and AdaptiveDownscale's grain-term cut through the renderer's store: snap on the delivered cut only, no snap on a fade); temporal detail takes the same cut / fade / fadeKeep when it is wired; matcher, LED, SRM later. Absent or stale: the consumer's own reduced path.

## Design goal, philosophy and decisions
- One cut / fade / flash judgement for every consumer (00-UNIVERSAL rules 1 and 20; doc 62 s1.2 U7). Consumers adapt to it as a registered fix with an owner before/after table (lead8\FIX-B6-D8.md), never a private variant.
- Multi-frame fade law, unit version 3 (2026-11-15, after U7-ACCURACY's version 2; source: refs\102b row U7-MULTIFRAME-FADE-LAW and lead16\attrib-d8-t1b.md, D8_SyntheticFade on e06026518: the pair law fired on 12 of 56 slow-fade frames; owner D8 target agreement >= 99.5 %, SceneMissing 0, owner-decisions 671/673 as cited in lead15\opus-audit-L-B6.md). Evidence is the accumulated gain of a monotone run, so no frame rate, fade length or window length enters (00-UNIVERSAL rule 6); the black window and the two-pair minimum are FadeFit's (M4) own. Hard cuts are unchanged. Owner ruling 2026-11-15 (relayed by lead 16, lead16 review-u7fade.md): the D8 target is agreement with the TRUTH (real cuts and fades), not with the retired private judges. Derivation and per-clip expectation: lead16 handover folder, file u7fade-report.md.
- Partial reset, unit version 3 (owner 2026-11-15 09:20: "PARTIAL reset on fades (no full restart mid-fade; history gently reduced so pre-fade detail is not carried into the new scene)"; 08:35: high firing bar, precision first). The reduction curve is the simplest principled one: the contrast ratio the laws already measure, multiplied per frame. Owner before/after table: lead18 handover folder, file c5-fix-u7fade.md (an owner question: the curve itself).
- Soft reset bar (owner 2026-11-15 08:35 EST, question window U7 Q4, refs\owner-decisions-2026-11-07.md: "need the bar fairly high here. Mid scene firing is a major problem and the motion vectors correct themselves fairly quickly even without a reset. The threshold for firing should be high and we need to make it as accurate as possible."; plan lead19\c5-softreset.md items 1-4): precision first. One named bar in this unit (no consumer keeps its own), set as high as a strength goes (1.0) until the D8 / U7DIAG truth run measures the soft resets on and off the labelled cuts (D8PARTIAL "soft" fields: soft resets, at / off truth cuts, mean graded strength on non-cut frames, eligible frames at or above 0.5 / 0.75 / 0.9 / the bar). A lower bar needs zero soft resets off the labelled cuts on the cut-free rows and the owner's look at the proposed value and its false-fire count. A provisional verdict never fires (it never delivers a cut either). Fades keep their own partial reset, unconditional.

## Status and fallbacks
No downgrade of its own. The Original (Bill) SceneDetector remains available as the original authority. Consumers' downgrades (all visible): a fade group without `fadeKeep` (a producer before v3) is a full restart in detail_pulse_stabilise / texture_restore (their "scene judge" fallback entry, `SceneFadeWithoutKeepWhy`) and in temporal detail ("reset: fade (no keep)"); a fade run rebuilt with reduced evidence is shown on the skeleton's "Skeleton scene" line.
